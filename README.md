## About

I'm **Heena Rather** — a Full Stack Developer and Freight Broker based in Dubai, UAE, working at the intersection of two industries that don't usually meet: global logistics and modern web development.

By day, I coordinate air, sea, and land freight across the GCC, Asia, and global trade corridors. Outside of that, I design and build digital products — from cinematic brand sites to interactive portfolios — combining a logistics operator's eye for detail with a developer's love of clean, purposeful UI.

- 🚢 Import/export operations, freight coordination, client & vendor management
- 💻 Full-stack web development — React, Next.js, Node.js
- 🎨 Premium, animation-driven front-end builds (GSAP, Three.js)
- 🎓 B.Tech in Electrical Engineering, Model Institute of Engineering and Technology
